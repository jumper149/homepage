= Building a Not-At-Home Studio: Introduction

[.normal]
Recently I've been making a lot of music and was working from home.
I'm living in a shared flat and was stuck in my bedroom a lot last year, which definitely was a bit frustrating.

This February I pulled the trigger and I'm now renting a small office to separate living and working.

image::room.jpg[What the room looks like now]

Now I'll write a short series on everything related to moving into this office since it's been quite a bit of work, but I'm also quite proud of the result.
I absolutely love walking to my office every morning and I can already tell that it has improved my quality of life signaficantly.

== The basic idea

There were already a few criteria to make it a nice office.

* Quiet during the day.
* Basic infrastructure like power, internet, lighting and heating.
* A restroom.
* A small kitchen. (optional)

But I also want to record and write music there!

* No noise complaints from sensitive neighbours.
* No EMI (would lead to buzzing guitars and audio gear).
* Acoustically viable room and the ability to add acoustic treatment.

It took some time, but luckily I found a place that's literally just 5 minutes walking distance from my home and surprisingly it checks most of the boxes.
